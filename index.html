John: "You always got a blunt, Doobie."

Doobie: "We can't always fight weed, John. We can't fight vapes, we can't fight dirty bong water. We can't fight nothin'. My whole life, all I ever did was smoke."

John: "Then give up, Doobie."

Doobie: "But I can't give up neither, I can't put down my own bong. That's the paradox, John... you see?"

John: "Then I have to green out on you."

Doobie: "When I green out, they'll just find another stoner... they have to... because they have to justify their nicotine."

Doobie: "Our time has passed, John..."
